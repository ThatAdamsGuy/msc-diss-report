# Dissertation Completion Plan

## Core argument

The dissertation develops a transparent deterministic model that compares two counterfactual pit stop scenarios over a common time window, implements it as a historic-analysis tool, verifies the implementation, validates it against selected races, and evaluates parameter sensitivity and limitations.

## Minimum complete dissertation (must have)

- Clear research gap, aim, objectives, scope and success criteria.
- Unambiguous scenario timeline and comparison window.
- Defined lap time model, cumulative gain equation and sign convention.
- Explicit treatment of pit-loss symmetry and rejoin traffic.
- Reproducible parameter/data provenance.
- Working software capable of running at least the minimum model.
- Independent hand-calculation and software verification.
- Transparent historic case-selection/data-preparation method.
- At least one successful and one unsuccessful/neutral validation case.
- Quantitative sensitivity analysis of the main parameters.
- Evidence-based discussion, limitations and conclusion.

## High-value additions

- Warm-up and traffic extensions.
- Relative driver/car pace treatment.
- Used-tyre support.
- Annotated race traces showing prediction and actual outcome.
- Parameter interactions and uncertainty ranges.

## Lower-priority additions

- Explicit overcut engine.
- Multi-car/probabilistic whole-race simulation.
- Live timing integration.
- Formal usability study.
